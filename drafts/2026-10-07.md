## Models & Research
*(section image: media asset `53460640` — neural core)*

**OpenAI publishes 722 AI-generated math manuscripts.** OpenAI released a public repository of 722 mathematics manuscripts, grouped into 372 families of related results, that it says were produced by an unreleased internal frontier model. The company reported the model was posed roughly 4,000 problems and that an average result used about three hours of ChatGPT Pro–level compute, and said it is "working to responsibly release" the model behind the work. The release includes formal Lean proofs for some results, and mathematicians have begun debating how AI-generated proofs at this scale should be verified.
Source: https://www.unite.ai/openai-releases-722-math-manuscripts-from-an-unreleased-ai-model/

**Google ships Nano Banana 2.1, cutting image-generation costs.** Google made Nano Banana 2.1, its efficiency-focused Gemini image model, generally available across the Gemini app, Google Search's AI Mode, Google Ads and developer tools including AI Studio. The update adds 4K output, reference tracking across up to 14 images and improved text rendering, and lists at about $0.0336 per 1,000 images in the Gemini API — roughly half the price of Nano Banana 2. Google set the older model's shutdown for October 29.
Source: https://decrypt.co/380257/google-launches-nano-banana-2-1

## Products & Platforms
*(section image: media asset `8d921438` — assistant orb / cloud)*

**xAI opens Grok Bot to enterprises with new governance controls.** xAI began offering Grok Bot, its autonomous "AI teammate" product, to enterprise buyers, adding admin and security audit logs, an off-by-default action-recording feature with 90-day retention, and OpenTelemetry export to customer monitoring stacks. The company named Legora, Supermicro and ServiceTitan among early customers, citing uses such as sales, recruiting and finance bots. Separately, xAI's Grok 4.7 became available through Google's enterprise agent platform.
Source: https://www.unite.ai/xai-launches-grok-bot-always-on-ai-teammates-with-their-own-cloud-computers/

**Anthropic expands its Claude Startups program.** Anthropic broadened its Claude Startups program to offer a free year of Claude Team with up to five premium seats and $1,000 in API credits, plus access to its Claude marketplace and office hours with its applied-AI team. Eligibility covers companies founded within the past five years or funded within the past two. The expansion comes as rival labs compete with their own startup-credit offers to win early developer adoption.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/anthropic-gives-startups-a-free-year-of-enterprise-service-and-1000-in-token-credits/

## Industry & Business
*(section image: media asset `7d5206ee` — capital into neural net)*

**DeepSeek nears a $12B raise ahead of a 2027 IPO.** DeepSeek is close to raising at least 80 billion yuan (about $12 billion), according to Bloomberg, overshooting an initial target of roughly 50 billion yuan after investor demand outstripped expectations. Tencent and battery maker CATL are among the largest committed investors, and the round is positioned ahead of a planned initial public offering in early 2027. It would be the company's largest external financing to date, following a smaller round reported earlier this year.
Source: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-06/deepseek-to-raise-at-least-12-billion-in-tencent-backed-funding

**Moonshot AI lines up a pre-IPO round near a $50B valuation.** Chinese lab Moonshot AI, backed by Alibaba, is in talks for a pre-IPO funding round that would value it at as much as $50 billion, up from roughly $35 billion in a recent raise, according to reporting from the South China Morning Post and others. The company confidentially filed for a Hong Kong listing in early September, with CICC and Goldman Sachs as underwriters. Investor interest has climbed since the mid-year release of its open-weight Kimi K3 model.
Source: https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3363026/chinas-moonshot-ai-aims-us50b-round-year-end-hong-kong-ipo-targeted-sources
