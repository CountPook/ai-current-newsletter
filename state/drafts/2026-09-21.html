Models & Products

StepFun opens API access to Step 5 Preview, a 600B-parameter model. On September 20, the Chinese lab StepFun made Step 5 Preview available through its API — a sparse mixture-of-experts model with roughly 600 billion total parameters and about 27 billion active per token, a 1-million-token context window, and image-and-text input. StepFun lists pricing at $1 per million input tokens and $2.70 per million output tokens and positions the model for software engineering, professional knowledge work and finance. The company said it plans to release the model's open weights on October 15. Source

OpenAI adds Chrome extensions to ChatGPT's desktop browser. On September 18, OpenAI said users of the ChatGPT desktop app's built-in browser on macOS and Windows can now install, pin and use Chrome extensions — such as password managers — directly inside the in-app browser. The update shipped alongside general availability of ChatGPT for Microsoft Word and support for multiple accounts. Source

Research

Anthropic says Claude sped up 30+ open-source biology models. In research published September 17, Anthropic reported that Claude, working within its Claude Science setup, optimized more than 30 open-source biomolecular models in under four weeks, making them roughly 4x faster on average, and added a low-memory mode for predicting large biomolecular systems on a single GPU node. Anthropic open-sourced the optimized code and, with lab-automation company Adaptyv Bio, launched a protein-design competition backed by $1 million in experimental validation and $1 million in Claude credits. Applications run through September 24. Source

Industry & Business

Anthropic and Accenture form an embedded-evaluation partnership. Announced September 18, the two companies said they will each invest at least $1 billion over five years to place a team of independent evaluators — led by Accenture's Faculty unit — inside Anthropic to red-team models, run alignment assessments and test safeguards. Anthropic framed the deal as part of a commitment to embed outside evaluators and said the arrangement is non-exclusive, with other evaluators to be named later. Source

Manus seeks a $4B valuation in a $500M round after its Meta deal collapsed. TechCrunch reported September 18 that AI-agent startup Manus is raising about $500 million at a roughly $4 billion valuation — about double the $2 billion Meta had agreed to pay before Chinese regulators blocked the acquisition. Reported potential investors include IDG Capital, Boyu Capital and CATL, alongside existing backers; Manus is said to be weighing a restructuring ahead of a possible Hong Kong IPO. Source

Lawsuit accuses OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and SpaceXAI of colluding to slow AI. A proposed class action filed September 18 in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California alleges the four companies illegally agreed to decelerate frontier AI development, reducing the value of paid subscriptions to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok. The complaint cites public statements around September 12 — after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's essay urging industry-wide caution — as evidence of coordination; the plaintiffs say the labs may slow down individually but not by agreement.
