AI vendors spent the last day pushing agents from demo to default. OpenAI's DevDay put always-on “Dots” agents and in-ChatGPT apps front and center, Meta aimed a new agent at small businesses, and Google DeepMind and academic labs posted research milestones in watermarked biology and game-playing AI — while the capital behind it all kept climbing. Here's the rundown →

Models & Research

Google DeepMind watermarks AI-designed proteins. Google DeepMind introduced SynthID Bio, a method that embeds hidden, verifiable signatures into AI-designed protein sequences and their predicted structures. In work published September 30, the team said it synthesized protein binders that remained functional in the lab while still carrying a detectable watermark, which it describes as a first. DeepMind is open-sourcing the code, data, and model weights, framing the tools as a provenance layer to support gene-synthesis screening and biosecurity. Source

webAI releases phone-sized reasoning models. webAI released TwiL-LM, a family of compact “formal-logic” reasoning models that includes a roughly 3.66-billion-parameter version the company says runs on a phone. webAI reports the small models outperform far larger systems on several reasoning benchmarks — citing 95.4% on BBH-logic and 64.1% on MuSR — and says the family passed half a million downloads in its first month. The weights ship in GGUF form for local machines under a non-commercial license. Source

A new AI tops human Stratego champions on a smaller training budget. Researchers from MIT, Carnegie Mellon, NYU, and Stanford reported a Stratego-playing system called Ataraxos that beat top human players while training on a fraction of the data earlier systems required. In a paper published in Nature on September 30, they said Ataraxos went 39–2 against elite players at a world championship and surpassed DeepMind's earlier DeepNash using roughly one-hundredth of the training examples. The approach pairs self-play reinforcement learning with decision-time planning that explicitly estimates hidden opponent pieces, and it was trained on 16 Nvidia H100 GPUs over about a week. Source

Products & Launches

OpenAI debuts always-on “Dots” agents and apps inside ChatGPT. At its DevDay event on September 29, OpenAI introduced “Dots,” always-on agents meant to carry out multi-step tasks on a user's behalf, and said developers can now build full applications that run inside ChatGPT and are distributed through OpenAI. The company also rolled out GPT-6.1 Sol, about a week after the prior Sol release. Executives used the event to reiterate that a public listing has been pushed back. Source

Meta points an AI agent at small businesses. Meta launched Muse for Small Business on September 29, an AI agent that connects to tools such as Asana, Slack, Zoom, Intuit, Box, and Canva, along with a business's ad accounts and Instagram and Facebook profiles. Meta pitches it as an operations layer that can analyze campaigns and sales, help manage cash flow and inventory, and draft customer messages and ads. The release accompanied a new Meta Enterprise Platform unit that sells the company's models, agents, and APIs to businesses and developers. Source

Industry & Funding

OpenAI is said to seek $30B at a ~$1.4T valuation. OpenAI is in early talks to raise at least $30 billion in a round that would value the company at about $1.4 trillion, according to reports on September 29. The financing is described as a bridge in place of an IPO, which CEO Sam Altman has delayed. The company closed a $122 billion round in March at an $852 billion post-money valuation, anchored by SoftBank, Amazon, and Nvidia. Source

Nvidia says each gigawatt is worth more with Vera Rubin. Nvidia said its upcoming Vera Rubin platform raises the revenue it can earn per gigawatt of deployed AI capacity to roughly $40 billion, up from about $25 billion for the current Blackwell generation and $18 billion for Hopper, according to disclosures reported September 30. The company also pointed to Anthropic as a major customer, citing about 2.5 gigawatts of contracted Nvidia capacity scheduled through 2028. The figures underscore how a small number of large AI labs are underwriting Nvidia's data-center roadmap. Source
