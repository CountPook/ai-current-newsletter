## Intro
The past few days leaned toward money and infrastructure rather than new frontier models. Investors committed billions to AI data centers, chips and agent tooling, while Perplexity moved more of its agent's work onto the device. Here's the rundown →

## Models & Products
**Perplexity turns on "Hybrid Compute" on the Mac.** On September 1, Perplexity switched on a mode for its Mac app that splits each task its agent runs between frontier models in the cloud and a smaller model running locally on the machine. When the agent reaches a sensitive file — the company cites medical records, tax returns and legal documents — that step is handed to the on-device model so the data stays on the Mac, and Perplexity open-sourced the classifier that decides what stays local. The mode requires at least 24GB of unified memory, limiting it initially to higher-end Macs. — https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/01/perplexity-launches-privacy-minded-hybrid-compute-ai-feature-for-mac/

## Infrastructure & Compute
**Crusoe raises more than $3B at a roughly $30B valuation.** AI data-center developer Crusoe raised over $3 billion in a round that values it at about $30 billion, co-led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners with participation from Mubadala Capital, according to reports on September 3. The valuation is roughly triple the more than $10 billion mark set by its Series E in late 2025. Crusoe, which builds and operates data centers for customers including OpenAI, recently signed a five-year, roughly $13 billion contract to supply GPUs and cloud capacity to Jane Street. — https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/crusoe-reportedly-raises-3b-at-a-30b-valuation/

**Gimlet Labs raises $300M at a $3B valuation for a "multi-silicon" inference cloud.** On September 4, Gimlet Labs said it raised $300 million in a Series B led by Andreessen Horowitz, with new backers Arm and Microsoft's M12 fund, valuing the startup at $3 billion. Its inference cloud routes each AI workload to whichever chip suits it best — spanning Nvidia, AMD, Intel, Arm, Cerebras and d-Matrix — and the company claims the approach can run frontier workloads three to ten times faster at the same cost and power. Gimlet said it is also working with Arm to make its software compatible with that company's chip designs. — https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/04/3356707/0/en/now-valued-at-3-billion-gimlet-labs-raises-300-million-in-series-b-led-by-andreessen-horowitz-for-industry-s-first-multi-silicon-inference-cloud-for-agentic-ai.html

## Funding & AI Security
**AIR emerges from stealth with $50M to build a "firewall" for AI agents.** Security startup AIR came out of stealth on September 1 with $50 million in seed funding co-led by Sequoia Capital and Greenoaks. Its product continuously inspects the skills, plugins and MCP servers that an organization's AI agents connect to, before and after deployment, and blocks ones it judges unsafe; the company says it filters out roughly a quarter of the agent add-ons it reviews. Founders Yair Saban and Niv Hoffman previously served in Israel's Unit 8200 signals-intelligence corps. — https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/air-raises-50m-to-help-companies-vet-the-skills-and-add-ons-ai-agents-use/

**Tripo AI raises about 3B yuan (~$446M) for 3D foundation models.** Chinese 3D-generation company Tripo AI said it raised roughly 3 billion yuan, about $446 million, across Series B and Series B+ rounds to scale "3D-native" foundation models and the data and compute beneath them. Alongside the raise, Tripo previewed Tripo P2.0, which it says can generate clean quad-topology meshes directly — output that is easier to edit, rig and animate than typical AI-generated 3D. Investors included Perfect World, CICC and CMC Capital. — https://www.3dnatives.com/en/tripo-ai-raises-3-billion-yuan-04092026/

**Félix Pago raises $200M for WhatsApp-based remittances and AI finance.** Félix Pago, a Miami-based service that lets Latin American workers in the U.S. send money home through a WhatsApp chat, raised $200 million in a Series C that values it at about $1.4 billion — roughly $87 million in equity led by Andreessen Horowitz plus about $113 million in credit from General Catalyst. The company says it has moved more than $8 billion for over 6 million users and plans to expand beyond remittances into AI-powered financial services, including an assistant embedded in the same chat thread. — https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/fintech-whatsapp-remittance-startup-felix-raises-200m-a16z-general-catalyst/
