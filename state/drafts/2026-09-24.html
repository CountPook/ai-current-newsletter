The frontier moved on price today: OpenAI and Anthropic both shipped cheaper models within hours of each other, while Xiaomi and Alphabet's Intrinsic pushed major systems into open source. Here's what happened in AI over the past day.

Model Releases

OpenAI ships two cheaper GPT-6 models. OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, positioned as lower-cost tiers of its current flagship family. The company said API prices for the new models are roughly half those of the previous tier — Luna is listed at $0.10/$0.50 per million input/output tokens — while carrying over accuracy, coding and tool-use gains from its larger Astra model. Sol and Luna are available through the API, and Luna is being offered to free ChatGPT users. (TechCrunch)

Anthropic releases Claude Opus 5.5. Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5, the first model in its 5.5 line, priced at $4/$20 per million input/output tokens — about 20% below Opus 5. The company said the model runs typical workloads roughly 40% cheaper and more than 30% faster than its predecessor, and matches or beats its larger Fable model on several benchmarks. Opus 5.5 is available on the Claude API, AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure, with Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 expected in the following weeks. (TechCrunch)

Xiaomi open-sources the omnimodal MiMo-V2.6 family. Xiaomi released its MiMo-V2.6 models under an MIT license, led by MiMo-V2.6-Pro, a 1.02-trillion-parameter sparse mixture-of-experts model with 42B active parameters, alongside a 309B Flash variant. Both are natively omnimodal — accepting text, image, video and audio — with 1-million-token context windows, and weights for Pro, Flash and a 9B distillation are posted on Hugging Face. Xiaomi reported a 46.32 score for Pro on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and said the API is live on its own platform and on OpenRouter. (SiliconANGLE)

Open Source & Robotics

Alphabet's Intrinsic open-sources its robotics stack. Intrinsic, the robotics unit inside Alphabet, released Intrinsic Core — a set of ROS-compatible building blocks for physical-AI applications — on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license at ROSCon 2026 in Toronto. The release bundles a hardware-agnostic real-time control framework, motion and grasp planning, pose estimation, a digital twin, simulation and preconfigured hardware drivers, drawn from software Intrinsic uses in manufacturing deployments. The company said the components can run on local hardware and are meant to reduce the integration work robotics teams build from scratch. (The Robot Report)

Industry & Funding

Ex-Anthropic startup Mirendil in talks for a $1B raise. Mirendil, an AI startup founded by former Anthropic researchers, is in talks to raise as much as $1 billion at a $5 billion valuation, according to reports. Kleiner Perkins is in discussions to lead the round, with Andreessen Horowitz also weighing participation. The talks come roughly three months after a $200 million seed round that valued the company at about $1 billion. (Bloomberg)

Policy & Governance

AI leaders brief the UN Security Council. The UN Security Council held a high-level briefing on artificial intelligence and international security on September 23, convened by France — which holds the Council's September presidency — and chaired by French foreign minister Jean-Noël Barrot. Briefers included OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, AI researcher Yoshua Bengio and Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue, who urged governments to adopt international standards and cooperation on frontier AI. Amodei proposed narrow global agreements such as barring AI-assisted bioweapons, verification systems so countries can check each other's commitments, and shared testing standards paired with an incident-notification system. (CNN)
