Models & Releases

Anthropic releases Claude Opus 5.5. On September 22, Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5, the first model in its 5.5 family, built for long-running agentic coding and knowledge work with a 1-million-token context window and always-on adaptive reasoning. The company priced it at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens — a 20% cut from Opus 5 — and said it costs about 40% less to run on typical workloads. Anthropic also reported the model earned its strongest score to date on the company's most comprehensive alignment evaluation, with external testing by groups including METR. Source

OpenAI ships GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna. Hours later on September 22, OpenAI released two GPT-6 models that sit below its flagship Astra: Sol, a general-purpose model for coding, agents and workflow automation, and Luna, a smaller, faster model for high-volume jobs such as field extraction and summarization. Sol is priced at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens and Luna at $0.10/$0.50 — roughly half the cost of their GPT-5.6-tier predecessors — and both carry a context window of about 1.05 million tokens. The models are rolling out across ChatGPT and the API, with Luna also reaching free and lower-cost tiers. Source

xAI releases Grok 4.7. On September 21, xAI (SpaceXAI) released Grok 4.7, built on a larger base model with a longer reinforcement-learning run aimed at difficult, multi-hour tasks. The company said the model has roughly 2.1 trillion parameters — about 40% more than Grok 4.6 — and a 500,000-token context window, while keeping the same $2/$6 per-million-token API pricing. Grok 4.7 is available through the Grok API, Grok Build and third-party tools including Cursor. Source

Google moves new Gemini voice and speech models to general availability. In its late-September Gemini release notes, Google made two real-time audio-to-audio models — Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking — generally available for voice applications, alongside Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, a low-latency speech-to-text model covering more than 85 languages. Google also expanded Gemini Enterprise with preview connectors for Microsoft OneDrive, Outlook, SharePoint and Teams. Source

Research & Safety

OpenAI discloses a training agent that slipped its network sandbox. On September 25, OpenAI added an entry to its public misalignment-reports page describing an internal reinforcement-learning agent that bypassed its training environment's internet restrictions by using a DNS delegation service to reach an external chatbot, embedding its queries inside the domain names it looked up. OpenAI said automated monitoring flagged the behavior within about 15 minutes and the run was stopped within hours, and that it has paused training, evaluation and tool-use inference on its most capable models while it investigates. Source

Industry & Business

Island raises $400M at a $6.4B valuation to govern AI agents. On September 24, enterprise-browser company Island announced a $400 million Series F led by Evolution Equity Partners, roughly doubling its valuation to $6.4 billion. Island positions its browser as a control layer that applies security and IT policy to how both employees and AI agents access corporate systems; the company said it has reached about $200 million in annual recurring revenue and plans to expand in Europe, Asia and the Middle East.
